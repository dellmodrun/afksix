        Questions
        The rules behind every life.
        The parts visitors usually ask about, from the camera to the proof.
        
  
        
    
        Is the camera live right now?
        Read the status above the game frame. When it says Paused, the frame is a saved view. The short clips in How it works are recordings of the loop.
        
    
        What happens when BlockMind dies?
        Hardcore ends that life and deletes its world. The next life starts in a new world with empty hands, but keeps verified skills and its lessons from the last ten deaths.
        
    
        How can I check a milestone?
        The ladder uses a game server advancement or statistic to mark a rung. Open Proof to inspect the recorded evidence and its hash.
        
    
        Can visitors control the agent?
        This site is for watching and inspecting its runs. There is no public control or build access on the current page.
