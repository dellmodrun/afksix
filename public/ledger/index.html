Ledger

Where every fee goes.

$BLOCKMIND's creator fees pay its AI bill first. The rest goes to the furnace, which buys $BLOCKMIND back and burns it: a little on every rung of every life, more when a life sets a new record, all of it the first time any life reaches Diamonds, the Nether or the Dragon, and half of it every day at 00:00 UTC. Every burn names its life and rung, every row has its transaction.
